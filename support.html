Last updated: October 2026

Need help? Email general@spinzonerecords.com and we'll get back to you.

Adding an album

There are three ways to get an album onto your shelf:

  • Share it from your music app. In Apple Music, tap Share on an album or song and choose Spinzone. In Spotify, tap Share, then More, then Spinzone — Spotify shows its own share screen first, so Spinzone appears one step in.
  • Paste a link. Tap Log on your shelf and paste a Spotify or Apple Music URL.
  • Text it. Send an album link by SMS to the Spinzone number and reply to the questions.

Sharing a single song works — Spinzone resolves it to the album it comes from, and marks that track as a standout.

Spinzone isn't in my share sheet

Open Spinzone once after installing; iOS registers an app's share extension on first launch. If it still isn't listed, scroll the app row in the share sheet to the end and tap More — newly installed extensions are sometimes switched off until you enable them there.

Signing in

Spinzone uses your phone number and a one-time code sent by text. There is no password. If the code doesn't arrive, check the number is correct and that you can receive SMS, then request it again.

Concerts

The Live tab tracks shows. Mark what you're going to, log what you went to, rate the venue and add photos. Your friends' plans appear alongside yours according to their calendar visibility.

Text messages

To stop all SMS from Spinzone, reply STOP to any message. You can also turn individual notification types on and off in the app under Profile.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your shelf, concerts, photos, lists, comments and friendships, and can't be undone. Albums other people found through you stay on their shelves, without your name attached.
