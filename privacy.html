Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Music Log SMS service ("the Service"), a personal tool for logging music listening history via SMS.

What We Collect

When you send a message to the Service, we collect the following information:

– Your phone number (used only to send a reply to your message)
– The album link or text you send
– The date and time of your message
– Your reply to follow-up questions (e.g., "Would you listen again?")

How We Use It

Data is used solely to log your personal music listening history to a private database. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to any third party, except as required to operate the Service (e.g., Twilio for SMS delivery) or as required by law.

Data Retention

Your data is stored in a private personal database and retained for as long as you choose to keep your listening log. You may request deletion at any time.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, reply STOP to any message from the Service, or contact the operator directly.
