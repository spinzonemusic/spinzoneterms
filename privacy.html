Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to Spinzone ("Spinzone", "we", "us") — the website at app.spinzonemusic.com, the Spinzone iOS app, and the SMS logging service that works alongside them. Together these are "the Service".

Spinzone is a place to keep a record of albums you listen to and shows you go to, and to share that with friends. This policy explains what we store, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

Information You Give Us

Account information. To create an account we require a mobile phone number, which is how you sign in — we send a one-time code by SMS. We also store your name, and a username used in your profile URL. You may optionally add an email address, a home city, and your preferred streaming service.

Your music activity. Albums you log or save to your shelf, the dates you listened, listen counts, whether it was a first listen, the format you listened on, notes you write, whether you would listen again, standout tracks you pick, and lists you create.

Your concert activity. Shows you mark as interested, going, or attended; the number of tickets; notes and ratings you write about a show or a venue; who you went with; and photos you upload from a show. You can also add a show manually by entering the artist, venue, city, date and ticket link yourself.

Social activity. Friend connections and requests, accounts you follow, albums you like, comments you write, and albums you share with another user along with any message you attach.

Links you paste. When you paste a concert or album link, we fetch that page to read the event or release details.

Information We Generate

  • A record of SMS messages sent to and from your phone number, including message content, used to operate and debug SMS logging and reminders.
  • Your notification preferences, per notification type.
  • Timestamps for account creation and updates, and for reminders we have already sent, so we do not send them twice.
  • When you add an album that another user shared or that appeared on their shelf, we record that it was discovered through them, so they can be credited.
  • Standard server and request logs kept by our hosting provider.

What We Do Not Do

Spinzone contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not build advertising profiles. We do not use your data to train machine learning models.

What Other Users Can See

Spinzone is partly social, so some information is visible to others by design:

  • Your name, username, and profile are visible according to your profile visibility setting (public or private).
  • Your concert calendar is visible according to your calendar visibility setting (everyone, or friends only). Friends can always see it.
  • Friends can see your shelf activity, comments, likes and logged shows.
  • When another user adds an album they found through you, your name may be shown to them as the source.

You control profile and calendar visibility at any time in your profile settings. Your phone number is never shown to other users.

Service Providers

We share information with a small number of providers strictly to operate the Service:

  • Vercel — website and app hosting, and storage for photos you upload.
  • Neon — the database where your account and activity are stored.
  • Twilio — delivery of SMS sign-in codes, reminders and notifications.
  • Resend — delivery of email, where you have given us an address.
  • Anthropic — when you paste a concert link, the content of that page is processed to extract the event details.

We also retrieve album and artist metadata from sources such as Spotify, MusicBrainz and Discogs. These lookups are about albums, not about you, and we do not send them your personal information.

We may disclose information if required by law.

Support Access

To investigate a problem you report, an administrator may view your account as you see it. This access is read-only — it cannot be used to post, change or delete anything in your account — and every such session is recorded in an audit log.

Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account at any time, from inside the app: Profile → Delete account. You do not need to email us or ask permission.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your account record, your shelf and listening history, your concerts and photos, your lists, comments, likes, shares, friendships, notifications and your SMS message history. It cannot be undone.

Two things deliberately survive deletion:

  • Albums other people discovered through you stay on their shelves, with the attribution to you removed. Deleting your account does not delete other people's shelves.
  • Shared reference records — concerts, venues, artists and album metadata — are not personal to you and other users depend on them, so they remain.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed as described above. Backups and hosting logs may persist for a short period after deletion before being overwritten in the ordinary course.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with our hosting and database providers. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Spinzone is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at general@spinzonerecords.com. To stop SMS messages, reply STOP to any message from Spinzone.
